Date: 2026-11-10

Method: candidates from jlcsearch.tscircuit.com; every row below was confirmed by reading its own jlcpcb.com/partdetail page, where "PCBA Type" reads "Economic and Standard".
Library column: Basic/Extended from jlcsearch `is_basic` and, where the page showed it, the partdetail heading. Stock: from the jlcsearch index; "n/a" means the index holds no stock figure (partdetail does not show stock either), so recheck stock on the order day.
Extended parts with `is_preferred` (150k C25755, 4x10k C25725) have no extended-part fee at JLC if flagged Preferred; not verified on the partdetail page.
Resistors are thick film, 1 % unless noted. Arrays are 5 %.
